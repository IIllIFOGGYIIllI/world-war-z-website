First purchase? Here's how checkout works

Normal items can be added to one cart (up to 50 of each product, subject to its stock and account limits). Review everything, choose your delivery location, then select Cash / Wallet or Protected Bank at checkout.

Vehicle rentals are different: buy them individually and choose a term from 1 to 30,000 server restarts. Final price and eligibility are checked before payment.

Orders may require a server restart for delivery. Check My Orders for the latest status.

Front-facing reference pattern. Pixel colours approximate armband textures, not actual game rendering.

  1. Export the JSON and add its filename to a local test server's cfgGameplay.json WorldsData.objectSpawnersArr.
  2. Verify all 49 armbands appear at the intended position, remain vertical and persist as expected after restart.
  3. From the front, confirm red is top-left, green top-right, blue bottom-left, orange bottom-right. If reversed, tick Mirror and re-export.
  4. Check whether tiles overlap, leave gaps, or produce unreadable colours. Adjust spacing and scale, then re-export.
  5. Keep your verified geometry settings. QR and billboard generation can reuse these values later.

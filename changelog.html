Release notes for the public World War Z Bot website and the dashboard experience.

21 August 2026

Version 1.22.96

Main Bot Community Tools

Added

  • Added an Owner-only Community Tools workspace with a secure webhook Embed Builder, persistent Sticky Messages and self-role panels.
  • The Embed Builder publishes through existing WWZ-managed webhook destinations while webhook URLs/tokens remain server-side.
  • Sticky Messages can use plain text or embeds and repost after configurable channel activity with a minimum anti-spam interval.
  • Self-role panels support up to 10 safe roles and can be edited, re-published or removed from the dashboard.

Safety & Compatibility

  • Administrative, moderation, managed and above-bot roles are excluded from self-role panels and rechecked at interaction time.
  • Pairs with Bot v1.18.104 and does not replace specialist polling or DayZ file-tooling services.

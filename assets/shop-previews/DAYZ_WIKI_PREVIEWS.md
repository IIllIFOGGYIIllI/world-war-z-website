# DayZ Wiki catalogue previews

Website v1.22.45 does not bundle or redistribute a copied DayZ Wiki image archive.
Instead, catalogue cards progressively resolve remote article/file thumbnails from
DayZ Wiki when an item has no manually configured HTTPS preview URL.

Source: https://dayz.fandom.com/
Item image category: https://dayz.fandom.com/wiki/Category:Item_images
Copyright information: https://dayz.fandom.com/wiki/DayZ:Copyrights

DayZ Wiki states that community content it can lawfully license is available under
CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 unless otherwise noted. DayZ/game artwork and other third-party
materials remain the intellectual property of their respective owners.

The World War Z website keeps local category SVGs as a fallback. A failed or
unavailable third-party image request must never block the shop, checkout,
delivery or Railway APIs.
